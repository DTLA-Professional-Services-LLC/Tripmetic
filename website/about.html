About Us

Bringing precision, logic, and professional financial control to global travel.

Our Origin Story

Most travel apps are built by tourists. Tripmetic was built by a financial controller.

With over 18 years of professional experience in financial management, reporting, and business forecasting, the foundation of Tripmetic was born out of a stark realization: the tools available for international travelers and digital nomads lacked fundamental financial logic. Travelers were forced to juggle disjointed spreadsheets, standalone currency calculators, and chaotic notes apps just to understand their daily burn rate.

We set out to fix that by treating personal travel logistics with the same rigor and precision required to run a successful enterprise. We engineered a platform that doesn't just track expenses, but actively calculates your remaining spending power across multiple global currencies in real-time.

Our Mission

Our mission is to eliminate the daily friction of international travel and remote work. Whether you are an executive scheduling meetings across three different timezones, or a digital nomad managing a strict multi-currency budget, Tripmetic is designed to be the ultimate, offline-resilient toolkit in your pocket.

We believe your focus should be on the experience of the destination, not the anxiety of the logistics.
