Global Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority, anywhere in the world.

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to Tripmetic ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy globally. This Privacy Policy applies to the Tripmetic mobile application (available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store) and our website (tripmetic.app).

1. Core Privacy Principles (App-Specific)

We designed the Tripmetic mobile application with privacy-by-design principles to protect your sensitive travel and financial planning:

  • Optional User Registration: You can use most features of the Tripmetic app without creating an account or logging in. Registration is entirely optional.
  • Local Storage by Default: Unless you explicitly choose to create an account and sync specific features, your personal travel itineraries, packing lists, and financial budget data are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us (via the App or our Website), we may collect the following information:

A. Account and Cloud Sync Data (Optional)

If you choose to create a Tripmetic account to back up your data or collaborate with others, we collect:

  • Account Details: Your email address and the secure authentication credentials used to create the account.
  • Cloud-Synced Features: Data specifically entered into the Bucket List and Group Travel Planner features is securely transmitted and stored on our cloud servers to enable cross-device syncing and collaboration.

B. Location Data (Foreground Only)

To provide our core travel toolkit features, Tripmetic requests access to your device's location services. We are completely transparent about how this data is handled:

  • Foreground Access Only: We only access your location while you are actively using the app (in the foreground). We do not track, collect, or access your location in the background when the app is closed or minimized.
  • Purpose of Collection: Your location data is used strictly to display your current position on the interactive map feature and assist with real-time navigation.
  • Data Storage: Location data is processed locally on your device in real-time. We do not transmit or store a history of your location data on our servers.

C. Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact Forms & Support: If you contact us via our website, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • Newsletters & Updates: If you opt-in to receive announcements, we collect your email address via our email service provider.

D. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website or use the app, certain information is collected automatically by third-party analytics and app store platforms. This includes:

  • Device Information: Operating system version, device model, and language settings.
  • Usage Data & Crash Logs: Anonymous data on how you navigate the app or website, and crash reports to help us fix bugs.
  • Website Analytics: IP addresses and browser types collected via our hosting provider (cPanel) and Google Analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information & Legal Basis

Under global data protection laws (including the GDPR), we must have a legal basis to process your data. We use the limited information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provide you with cloud-syncing capabilities for your Bucket List and Group Travel Planner if you choose to create an account.
  • Consent: To send administrative information or promotional materials (only if you have explicitly opted in via our website).
  • Legitimate Interest: To provide customer support, monitor app performance, fix technical issues (via crash logs), and analyze website traffic to improve our services.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to operate our website and improve our app. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics & Google Tools: Used for tracking website traffic and app usage statistics. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Apple App Store & Google Play Console: These platforms automatically collect aggregate, anonymous installation and crash data when you download and use the app.
  • Email Octopus: We use Email Octopus to manage our mailing lists and send newsletters. (Email Octopus Privacy Policy)
  • cPanel Analytics: Basic server-level tracking for website health and performance.

5. European (EEA), UK, and International Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), Canada, Brazil, Australia, or other jurisdictions with comprehensive data protection laws (such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, LGPD, or APPs), you are granted specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can request that we delete your personal information and cloud-synced account data from our systems.
  • Right to Restrict Processing & Object: You have the right to limit or object to how we process your data, including opting out of direct marketing.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. For data stored locally on your device (such as offline budgets or itineraries), you control that data directly by managing or uninstalling the app. For data synced to your optional cloud account (such as your bucket list or group travel plans), we can assist with data portability or deletion upon request.

6. United States Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of California (under the CCPA/CPRA), Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), or other US states with privacy laws, you have specific rights:

  • Right to Know: You can request details about the categories and specific pieces of personal data we collect.
  • Right to Delete: You can request that we delete your contact information and cloud-synced account data from our servers.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: We do not sell your personal information to third parties. You can opt out of any targeted marketing emails at any time.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

7. International Data Transfers

Tripmetic operates globally. If you use our website or opt into our communications, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries where our third-party providers operate. By using our services, you consent to these transfers. When transferring data from the EEA/UK to other countries, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) provided by our third-party processors.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information (like your account details or synced lists) only for as long as your account remains active or as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. Once the data is no longer necessary, or upon your request for account deletion, we securely delete or anonymize it.

9. Children's Privacy

Our app and website are not intended for children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions, such as the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in global legal requirements or app features. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us & Complaints

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, or if you wish to lodge a complaint, please reach out to us at:

Website Contact Form: tripmetic.app/contact.html
